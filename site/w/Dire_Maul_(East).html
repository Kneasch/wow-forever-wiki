Dire Maul (East) is a dungeon in WoW Forever for characters of level 54-60.

Location

Dire Maul, Feralas. Three entrances: east wall of the central courtyard, Eldreth Row at the Dire Maul entrance, and Lariss Pavilion NE of Camp Mojache (needs Crescent Key).
