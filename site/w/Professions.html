Professions

From the WoW Forever Wiki

Professions are trade skills. Each character can learn two primary professions, which are the gathering and production professions, and all three secondary professions. Professions are levelled from 1 to 300 in four ranks: Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert and Artisan.

WoW Forever adds camp features to every profession. These are placeable objects such as a Rock Garden or a Greenhouse, learned from blueprints that drop in the world and in dungeons. It also adds new materials, such as Heavy Thorium Ore, and new top-level recipes.
