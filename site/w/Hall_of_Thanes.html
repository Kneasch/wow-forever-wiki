Hall of Thanes is a new dungeon in WoW Forever for characters of level 13-20.

Location

Beneath Ironforge (Old Ironforge, under the Throne Room). Enter the king's hall at The High Seat, turn left to a spiderweb-covered passage at ~43.5, 52.0 and follow it down.

Bosses

  1. Faldrim Anvilmar
  2. Magmatus
  3. Plunder
  4. Durgen Dirgehammer
