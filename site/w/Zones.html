Zones

From the WoW Forever Wiki

World maps for every zone in WoW Forever: 44 zones, 6 cities, 4 battlegrounds and 2 continents. Select a map to open it full size.

5 maps are new in Forever: Darkspear Islands, Mount Hyjal, Riverglades, Shen'dralas and Zephras Isle. The rest are the Classic zones, drawn from the Forever client, so they show any changes Forever has made.
