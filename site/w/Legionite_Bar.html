Legionite Bar is an uncommon metal bar used as a crafting reagent. It is new in WoW Forever. Made by alchemists (Recipe: Transmute Legionite, Alchemy 275) from a Heavy Thorium Bar and a Fel Crystal. Used with Heavy Thorium Bars in Forever's level 60 Blacksmithing plans.
