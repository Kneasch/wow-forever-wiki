Mining is a gathering profession in WoW Forever. Mining is a gathering profession. Miners collect ore, stone and gems from mineral veins found across the world, and smelt ore into metal bars at a forge. Blacksmithing and Engineering use most of what miners gather.

Training

Mining is learned from a mining trainer in rank order. Each rank raises the maximum skill by 75.

RankSkillRequiresSpell
Apprentice1–75None2575
Journeyman1–150Mining 50, level 102576
Expert1–225Mining 125, level 203564
Artisan1–300Mining 200, level 3510248

Maximum skill comes from the Forever spell tooltips. The skill and level needed to train each rank are the Classic values and have not been confirmed for Forever.

  • Mining Pick: Must be in your bags to mine. Sold by trade supplies vendors.
  • Find Minerals (spell 2580): Shows nearby mineral veins on the minimap.
  • Smelting (spell 2656): Turns ore into bars. Needs a forge, or a Molten Foundry for Forever's top recipe.

Research notes

Where sources disagree:

  • Smelt Azerothium (Mining 275) and Smelt Heavy Thorium (Mining 300) come from fan guides. Their items (Pitchblende 249426, Azerothium Bar 249726, Heavy Thorium Ore 248815, Heavy Thorium Bar 251291) are on Wowhead, but the smelting spells were not found, so the skill levels are unconfirmed.
  • One guide says Blizzard reduced the number of Poor Copper Vein nodes in starter areas on 1 October (beta). No Poor Copper Vein object was found in the tooltip scan, which did turn up two new starter-zone nodes, Chunk of Amber and Large Chunk of Amber.

Not researched yet:

  • The tooltip API maps only the zone with the most spawns of each node object. Other zones a node appears in are not listed. The full zone lists are on the Wowhead object pages, which could not be read from this environment.
  • Chunk of Amber and Large Chunk of Amber (new in Forever, Mining 1) have no known contents yet.
  • Node contents: ore and stone are the Classic contents and gem drops are not listed (see nodes_contents_basis).
  • Recipe skill levels are the Classic values (skill_basis: classic).
  • Trainers and their locations are not researched. Guides name Zephras Isle trainers, but spell them differently.
  • Rank training costs are not researched.
  • Where Heavy Thorium Ore and the scarce materials (Pyrite, Bauxite, Pitchblende) are gathered is not known. Fan guides say scarce materials come from normal nodes as a bonus.
