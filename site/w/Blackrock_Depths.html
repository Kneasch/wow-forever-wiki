Blackrock Depths is a dungeon in WoW Forever for characters of level 52-60.

Location

Enter Blackrock Mountain, climb down the large chain to the central structure, descend via Forgewright's Tomb, then through the Grinding Quarry to The Masonry; the dungeon portal is in the far corner (Warcraft Tavern).
