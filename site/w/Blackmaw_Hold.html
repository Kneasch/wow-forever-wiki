Blackmaw Hold is a new dungeon in WoW Forever for characters of level 55-60.

Location

Entrance in Azshara inside a furbolg tunnel at 38.7, 33.4; the portal itself is on the Winterspring map at 66.6, 84.4 (Warcraft Tavern).
