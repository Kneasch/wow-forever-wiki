Scarlet Monastery Graveyard is a dungeon in WoW Forever for characters of level 30-38.

Location

Entrance to Scarlet Monastery is in the northeast corner of Tirisfal Glades; wing portals inside the main building (Warcraft Tavern). Graveyard is the leftmost open portal.

Bosses

  1. Interrogator Vishas
  2. Bloodmage Thalnos
  3. Ironspine (rare)
  4. Azshir the Sleepless (rare)
  5. Fallen Champion (rare)
  6. Scorn (rare)
