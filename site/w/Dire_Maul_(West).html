Dire Maul (West) is a dungeon in WoW Forever for characters of level 56-60.

  1. Location
  2. Bosses

Location

Dire Maul, Feralas: west wall of the central courtyard (two close entrances; Crescent Key required, looted from Pusillin in Dire Maul East). The Athenaeum connects West and North.

Bosses
