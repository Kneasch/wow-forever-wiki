Upper Blackrock Spire is a dungeon in WoW Forever for characters of level 59-60.

Location

Blackrock Mountain. Entered from Lower Blackrock Spire via the door upstairs on the left of the LBRS entrance; needs the Seal of Ascension (quest in LBRS).
