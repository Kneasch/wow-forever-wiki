Lower Blackrock Spire is a dungeon in WoW Forever for characters of level 55-60.

Location

Blackrock Mountain (Searing Gorge side per Warcraft Tavern). Portal reached via the door by the Blackrock Spire meeting stone, or by jumping from the balcony where Bodley stands.
