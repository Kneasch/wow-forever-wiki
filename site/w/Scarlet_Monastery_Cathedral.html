Scarlet Monastery Cathedral is a dungeon in WoW Forever for characters of level 38-46.

Location

Scarlet Monastery is in the northeast corner of Tirisfal Glades. The Cathedral portal is through the closed door on the left as you enter; the door needs The Scarlet Key, from a chest behind Arcanist Doan in the Library wing.

Bosses

  1. High Inquisitor Fairbanks
  2. Scarlet Commander Mograine
  3. High Inquisitor Whitemane
