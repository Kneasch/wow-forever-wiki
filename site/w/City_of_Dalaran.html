City of Dalaran is a new dungeon in WoW Forever for characters of level 28-35.

Location

Both factions enter through the sewers; Dalaran itself is in Alterac Mountains (Warcraft Tavern; its text also mentions Silverpine Forest for the sewer entrance, unresolved).
